ℹ️ ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Built to expose AI-generated images

As AI image generation becomes indistinguishable from real photography, knowing the truth matters. Reality Check makes authenticity detection instant, free, and accessible to everyone.

🔍

What is Reality Check?

Reality Check is an AI content authenticity analyzer that tells you whether an image was created by a human or generated by an AI tool like Midjourney, DALL-E, or Stable Diffusion.

Upload any image and get an instant confidence score from 0–100%, along with a plain-language explanation of what the detection engine found — no technical jargon, no sign-up required.

⚙️

How does it work?

1
You upload an image. JPG, PNG, or WEBP up to any reasonable size.
2
The image is sent to Sightengine's AI detection engine. It analyzes pixel patterns, texture consistency, lighting anomalies, and frequency signatures that are characteristic of AI-generated images.
3
A confidence score is returned. 0% means almost certainly real. 100% means almost certainly AI-generated.
4
Your image is immediately deleted. Nothing is stored on the server after analysis.
🔒

Privacy & limits

  • Images are deleted from the server immediately after analysis — never stored
  • No account, login, or personal data required
  • 5 free analyses per day per visitor to keep the service fair and sustainable
  • API keys and credentials are stored securely via environment variables — never in code
