About Reality Check

Built to help people identify AI-generated content quickly and accurately.

What is Reality Check?

Reality Check is an AI content authenticity analyzer that tells you whether an image was created by a human or generated by an AI tool like Midjourney, DALL-E, or Stable Diffusion. Upload any image and get an instant percentage score with a plain-language explanation.

Who built this?

Reality Check was built by Aylin, a Software Engineering student at Fırat University, Turkey. This is an independent project built from scratch as part of a journey into applied AI and web development.

How does it work?

When you upload an image, it is sent to an AI detection engine that analyzes pixel patterns, texture consistency, lighting anomalies, and frequency signatures that are characteristic of AI-generated images. The result is returned as a confidence percentage.
